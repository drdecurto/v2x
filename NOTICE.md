Road geometry and associated building polygons derive from OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License (ODbL): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Source bounds/URLs and hashes are preserved in the scenario source.json files. Derived road databases retain those attribution and database-license obligations. Added demand and sensor placements are synthetic, not municipal measurements.
