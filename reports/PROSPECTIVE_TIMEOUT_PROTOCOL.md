# Prospective timeout service protocol

This bounded follow-up replaces the retrospective completion oracle with an actual timer and future. Its scope is local service behavior, not city policy effects, radio delivery, capacity under load or field validation.

Twelve cases use new wording within familiar categories:3clean,6violating,3uncertain. Four additional copies of a clean advisory isolate stale evidence, a superseded observation, expiry and a passed decision point. These are prospective new-wording tests with constructed references, not independent unseen semantic families or blinded human labels. Model selection uses prior development data: localLlama3.1:8b andPhi4. No model is chosen after this test's outcomes.

Each model/case starts one genuine asynchronous inference future. The no-audit and template controls act at0s. The deadline observer waits up to2s and either uses the returned verdict with current guards or commits to a verified template. Both observers share the same request: this pairs service realizations but is not four independent deployed servers or a throughput comparison. Clock scheduling jitter is recorded rather than interpreted as hard real-time certification. The completed future is drained after decisions to preserve failures, timings and output; it cannot retroactively change them. No external cancellation or saved computation is claimed.
