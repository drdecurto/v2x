# Communication evidence audit

Checked against primary sources on 2026-10-30.

## Selected route: empirical LTE C-V2X sidelink traces
SEE-V2X, Ruoshen Mo, Bo Wu, Zhaowei Tan and Hang Qiu, SenSys 2025. DOI https://doi.org/10.1145/3715014.3722077 . Verified title and authors against the project site and publisher Crossmark. Sources: https://cisl.ucr.edu/SEE-V2X/ and https://github.com/UCR-CISL/SEE-V2X .

The downloaded outdoor-parking-lot archive contains ten parameter configurations with corresponding TX and RX logs. Match transmitter ID, sequence number and transmitted timestamp. Exclude inserted analysis rows (`extra=1`). Calculate delay from matched timestamps and cross-check against the supplied latency column. Compute delivery only within first-to-last matched transmission support because the receiver logging boundaries are not documented in these CSVs. Report excluded boundary transmissions and unmatched RX records. This estimates conditional in-window PDR, not whole-session delivery.

The 1470-byte, priority-0 profile is the initial sensitivity example. Packet sizes, priority and 20 ms offered interval are source settings; changing them requires a different trace or justified model. A few sparse application messages cannot be claimed to reproduce this high-rate load. Replay keeps channel conditions exogenous. Applying the profile to an infrastructure downlink is a transfer assumption and requires an explicit sensitivity analysis; these direct-link measurements do not certify V2I deployment performance.

Clock synchronization uncertainty must still be established from the paper. Agreement between two fields generated by one measurement pipeline does not independently validate clock accuracy. No dataset redistribution license was found in the inspected README; raw files remain local.

## Strong alternative
Berlin V2X: https://github.com/fraunhoferhhi/BerlinV2X . Verified paper DOI: 10.1109/VTC2023-Spring57618.2023.10200750. It includes cellular ping and sidelink packet data. Ping is round-trip latency: never silently divide by two and label the result measured one-way delay. Access to the IEEE Dataport download has not yet been established in this session.

## Other candidate limitations
NIST C-V2X interoperability datasets (https://data.nist.gov/od/id/mds2-3541) are useful packet-capture evidence, but receiver-only captures are insufficient to estimate packet loss without a known sent-packet denominator. Do not infer delay from packet interarrival time.

CRAWDAD means Community Resource for Archiving Wireless Data At Dartmouth, not 'At Demise'. A specific dataset, measurement schema and license are needed; the archive name alone supplies no calibration. SPMD should likewise not be assumed to expose synchronized per-packet TX/RX data without inspecting a specific release.

## Standards correction
3GPP TR 37.885 is 'Study on evaluation methodology of new Vehicle-to-Everything (V2X) use cases for LTE and NR': https://portal.3gpp.org/desktopmodules/Specifications/SpecificationDetails.aspx?specificationId=3209 . TR 37.910 is 'Study on self evaluation towards IMT-2020 submission': https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_tr/137900_137999/137910/19.00.00_60/tr_137910v190000p.pdf . Neither should be advertised as a ready-made stochastic application-delay distribution. Implementing a simple delay/loss wrapper is not equivalent to implementing a 3GPP PHY/MAC evaluation.

## Permissible framing
Measured LTE C-V2X trace-informed application benchmark with an edge/cloud inference prototype. Synthetic additional delays are labeled stress tests. Mac plus Nebius is a software laboratory, not a road trial or 5G/6G radio testbed.
